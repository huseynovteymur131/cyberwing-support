CyberWing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how CyberWing handles information. The developer responsible for the game is Teymur Huseynov, based in Azerbaijan, using the support name TBT Studios. Contact: tbt.studios.support@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

CyberWing stores scores, collection progress, inventory, Repair Kit balance, equipped items, language and audio preferences on your device. You can play without a CyberWing account. Device or operating-system backups may include local data. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove progress that has not been synchronized; recovery of device-only data is not guaranteed.

Optional accounts and cloud synchronization

You may sign in with Apple or Google to use a CyberWing account. Our authentication and cloud services are provided by Supabase. They process a game account identifier, linked Apple or Google identifiers, the email address supplied by the provider (which may be an Apple private relay address), authentication and session information, and service security logs such as IP addresses and request times. Your Apple or Google password is entered with that provider and is not received by CyberWing.

After you choose which progress to keep, we synchronize supported scores and collection progress with your account. Purchases, item entitlements and Repair Kit credits and spending are checked and recorded separately on the server to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate grants. A device identifier and a local record of confirmed offline Repair Kits allow purchased kits to be spent offline and reconciled when you reconnect. A kit reserved for one device is not simultaneously spendable on a second device. Not all preferences or device-only progress are cloud backups.

We use these data to authenticate you, maintain and recover supported progress, deliver purchased items, operate synchronization and prevent fraud. We do not publish your sign-in email or cloud save as a public profile or use account information for our own targeted advertising. Supabase hosts our current account database in the EU (Ireland); its service providers and authentication providers may process information in other countries. See Supabase Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and consent

CyberWing integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded advertisements that can provide a revive. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages advertising consent where applicable. During development, advertisements may be test advertisements; the advertising SDK still communicates with Google.

According to Google’s SDK disclosures, information processed may include IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostic and crash information, performance information, and app interactions. This information supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Available identifiers and advertising behavior depend on device permissions, consent, region, and SDK configuration. The game does not request access to precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, or photos for advertising.

Google processes information under its own policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where required, a consent message lets you accept, refuse, or manage advertising choices. When available, use “Ad Privacy Options” in the game’s Settings to revisit those choices. You may also manage advertising permissions in your device settings. Refusing consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay; availability of rewarded advertising can vary.

In-app purchases

On iOS, Apple processes in-app payments through the App Store. We receive and verify purchase information such as product and transaction identifiers, the purchase environment, signed transaction data and the association with your CyberWing account. We retain delivery and spending records to grant items, restore eligible purchases and prevent a transaction from being used more than once. We do not receive your payment card number or banking credentials. Prices are provided by the store for its region. TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment and do not charge real money.

Game Center leaderboard

Game Center on iOS is separate from your CyberWing account. When you connect Game Center, CyberWing submits your best score, including an existing local best, to Apple's leaderboard. Apple processes your Game Center player identifier, profile or nickname and score; other players may see your Game Center profile and ranking according to Apple's settings. Connecting Apple or Google to CyberWing does not by itself connect Game Center or create a shared Android leaderboard. Manage your Game Center profile and privacy choices in your Apple device settings. See Game Center and Privacy.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer questions and resolve issues. Avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information. Support email is handled through Gmail and may be processed by Google.

Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until removed or reset. Cloud account data and supported saves are retained while your account is active. To request account deletion in the game, open Settings → Account → Manage Account → Delete Account and complete the confirmation and provider verification. Signing out or uninstalling the game does not delete your cloud account. When deletion succeeds, the account and associated cloud progress, wallet and entitlements are deleted. A limited transaction record without the deleted CyberWing user identifier is retained to prevent replay and duplicate delivery; this is not an account backup. Account deletion does not automatically refund App Store purchases or delete Apple's Game Center records. Contact support if you cannot access the deletion flow.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations, then delete it. Apple, Google, Supabase and hosting providers determine retention for data they process under their policies. Authentication tokens are stored using platform secure storage where supported; server access is restricted and transfers use HTTPS. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

International processing and privacy rights

Apple, Google, Supabase, hosting and email providers may process information outside your country of residence. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us about information we hold and use the relevant provider's privacy controls for information it processes. Device-only progress that has never been synchronized is not available on our server.

Children

CyberWing is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support correspondence. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
