CyberWing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how CyberWing handles information. The developer responsible for the game is Teymur Huseynov, based in Azerbaijan, using the support name TBT Studios. Contact: tbt.studios.support@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

CyberWing stores scores, collection progress, inventory, Repair Kit balance, equipped items, language and audio preferences locally on your device. The current version does not require a player account or upload this progress to a developer-operated server. Device or operating-system backups may include local data. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove progress; recovery is not guaranteed.

Advertising and consent

CyberWing integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded advertisements that can provide a revive. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages advertising consent where applicable. During development, advertisements may be test advertisements; the advertising SDK still communicates with Google.

According to Google’s SDK disclosures, information processed may include IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostic and crash information, performance information, and app interactions. This information supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Available identifiers and advertising behavior depend on device permissions, consent, region, and SDK configuration. The game does not request access to precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, or photos for advertising.

Google processes information under its own policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where required, a consent message lets you accept, refuse, or manage advertising choices. When available, use “Ad Privacy Options” in the game’s Settings to revisit those choices. You may also manage advertising permissions in your device settings. Refusing consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay; availability of rewarded advertising can vary.

Purchases and leaderboards

The current development version uses simulated test purchases and does not process real payments. It does not currently submit scores to an online leaderboard. This policy will be reviewed before real purchases or additional online services are enabled.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer questions and resolve issues. Avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information. Support email is handled through Gmail and may be processed by Google.

Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed or reset. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations, then delete it. Google determines retention for data it processes under its policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

International processing and privacy rights

Google and email providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us about information we hold; use Google’s privacy controls for information processed by Google. We cannot retrieve device-only progress from a developer server.

Children

CyberWing is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support correspondence. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
