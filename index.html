CyberWing Support

Need help with CyberWing? Email tbt.studios.support@gmail.com.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, operating-system version, game version, what happened, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment information.

Progress and inventory

CyberWing keeps local progress and offers optional Apple or Google sign-in for supported cloud progress. Confirm synchronization before reinstalling; device-only data may be lost. For eligible App Store items, use Restore Purchases in the shop while connected to the same CyberWing account. Repair Kits already delivered for offline use are reserved to their device and are not duplicated by restoring purchases.

Delete an account

Open Settings → Account → Manage Account → Delete Account. Complete the confirmation and provider verification. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete a cloud account. Contact support if you cannot complete deletion. Deleting an account does not request a refund or delete Apple's Game Center records.

Advertising choices

When available, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review advertising consent. Rewarded ads may be unavailable when offline or when an ad cannot be loaded.
