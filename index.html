CyberWing Support

Need help with CyberWing? Email tbt.studios.support@gmail.com.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, operating-system version, game version, what happened, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Do not send passwords or payment information.

Progress and inventory

Progress is currently stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase it. Please contact support before reinstalling if you are investigating a problem with your progress.

Advertising choices

When available, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review advertising consent. Rewarded ads may be unavailable when offline or when an ad cannot be loaded.
